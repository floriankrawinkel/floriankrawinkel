Projects

Fruit Quest (WIP)

Teaching children about the importance of a healthy diet is essential for their future. This serious game for elementary school students aims to do this through gamification—not only within the game itself, but also through collectible figures that parents can 3D print and use as action figures or fridge magnets.

Clicking here will take you to the project's page, where you can find more information about the project and its development process.

UPAT

UPAT (Unseen Patient Attention Training) is a VR attention training program for doctors and medical staff that I created for my bachelor's thesis. It's designed to facilitate research studies and is currently being used by researchers at Technische Hochschule Köln to investigate the potential effects of Visual Delegates in XR trainings.

Clicking here will take you to the project's page, where you can find more information about the project and its development process.

P.A.U.L.A.

PAULA stands for Pump Action User Learning Application and is an immersive XR training program for firefighters, developed in collaboration with the Cologne Fire Department. It allows trainees to practice pump operations without requiring an additional vehicle or water.

Clicking here will take you to the project's page, where you can find more information about the project and its development process.

Microwave Minigames

Microwave Minigames was created as a submission for the GMTK Game Jam 2026. Based on the theme of "Countdown," me and a close friend developed a Wario Ware–style game that uses different input systems to count down numbers to zero.
